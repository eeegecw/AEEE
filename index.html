01 / WHO WE ARE

Connecting ideas.
Powering possibilities.

AEEE is the student association of the Electrical and Electronics Engineering department at Government Engineering College Wayanad. We bring students together through technical activities, events, projects and shared experiences beyond the classroom. Our team works to create a space where every student can learn, contribute and grow.
