Privacy

This website provides information about the Association of Electrical & Electronics Engineers at Government Engineering College Wayanad.

If you contact the association through the contact form, the information you choose to submit is used to receive and respond to your message. The form is handled through the service configured on the contact page.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information through the website.

For questions about a message you have submitted or a privacy-related request, please use the AEEE contact page.
